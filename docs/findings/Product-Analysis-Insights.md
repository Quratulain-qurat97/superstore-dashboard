## Product Analysis — Key Findings

- **Overall Profit Margin:** 12.47%, with three loss-making sub-categories.
- **Tables recorded the largest sub-category loss:** -$17,725.48, at a -8.56% profit margin.
- **Copiers generated the highest sub-category profit:** $55,617.82, while Labels achieved the highest profit margin at 44.42%.
- **Deep discounting was associated with negative profitability:** Transactions with discounts above 20% generated a loss of $135,376.06, compared with $421,773.08 profit from transactions discounted by 20% or less.
- **High-discount transactions represented only 13.9% of transactions**, yet had an aggregated profit margin of -37.32%.
- **Product-level analysis covered 1,862 distinct products**, generating $2,297,200.86 in sales and $286,397.02 in profit.
- **Technology led category sales in 2017**, reaching $271,730.81.
